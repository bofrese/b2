# Styles/movements → business fit

Activation index for style selection and business-fit mapping (Phase B2). Match logic at the foot.
**Verify trend claims at build time.** For emerging/2026 trends see `trends.md`.

- **Minimalism** - whitespace, restraint. SaaS, fintech, premium/luxury, professional services. Signals clarity/trust.
- **Swiss / International Typographic** - grid, Helvetica-ish, objective. Corporate, editorial, design-conscious B2B. Signals rigor.
- **Editorial** - magazine layout, strong type hierarchy, asymmetry. Publishing, agencies, thought-leadership, DTC storytelling.
- **Bento grid** - compartmentalized cards (Apple-style). SaaS/tech feature showcases, dashboards, product marketing. 2026 mainstream.
- **Brutalism / neo-brutalism** - raw, high-contrast, borders, no soft shadows, "engineered." Creative studios, dev tools, web3, youth/fashion, anti-corporate brands. 2026 counter-trend to bento ("tactile brutalism").
- **Maximalism** - dense, layered, expressive color/type. Fashion, music, culture, bold consumer brands. Anti-SaaS.
- **Glassmorphism** - frosted blur/transparency/depth. Use sparingly - nav, modals, overlays. Tech, fintech dashboards, Apple/Windows-adjacent. Overuse = perf + legibility cost.
- **Neumorphism** - soft extruded monochrome. Niche; low contrast = a11y risk. Rarely a full-site choice; small tactile UI moments only.
- **Dark-mode-first** - dark as primary canvas, neon/gradient accents. Dev tools, gaming, crypto, AI products, media. Signals technical/premium.
- **Retro / Y2K** - chrome, gradients, pixel, nostalgia. Gen-Z consumer, fashion, music, entertainment.
- **Organic / bio / hand-drawn** - soft blobs, natural texture, warmth. Wellness, sustainability, food, nonprofits, healthcare. Signals human/approachable.
- **Corporate memphis (flat blob illustration)** - **fatigued/avoid** unless deliberately ironic; reads generic-startup.

**Match logic:** audience expectation × brand personality × conversion goal. Regulated/trust (finance, health, legal) → restraint. Creative/differentiation → brutalism/maximalism/editorial. Ecommerce → clarity + product focus + fast. Nonprofit → warmth + story + accessibility.
